About Bear Services

A roll-off bin company based in Orem for about 20 years, giving Utah County builders, businesses and homeowners a dumpster at a fair price. Part of Dunn Companies, which owns the landfill your load goes to.

Who we are

Bear Services, also known as Bear Bins Utah, is a roll-off dumpster service based at 581 W 1600 N in Orem. For about 20 years we have provided commercial, construction and residential roll-off bins to Utah County at an affordable price, and we've made getting a bin fast and easy the whole time. Bins for builders working a single lot or a whole subdivision, standing rotations for warehouses and shops, and driveway drops for homeowners clearing a garage or remodeling a kitchen.

Why we can bill you on real weight

Most dumpster companies charge a flat rate per bin and hope the load comes in light. We weigh every bin and charge you for what was actually in it, with the first ton included in the delivery and removal price. We can do that because Bear is part of Dunn Companies, which also owns and operates the Five Mile Pass C&D landfill and the Five Mile Pass sand and rock quarry. Your load goes over our own scale into our own landfill, and the number on the scale is the number on your bill.

How we work

  • One number does everything: call or text (801) 785-4494 and you reach a person, not a phone tree. Texts are answered outside business hours too.
  • Two bins: a 15 yard (12,000 lbs) and a 30 yard (24,000 lbs). Tell us the job and we'll size it.
  • 14 days before a daily rental fee starts. Finish early, text, and the bin is gone.
  • Short-term, long-term, one-time or a standing rotation.

Where we are and where we go

The yard is in Orem, and the trucks run Orem, Provo, Vineyard, Lehi, American Fork, Pleasant Grove, Lindon, Springville, Spanish Fork, Saratoga Springs, Eagle Mountain and the rest of Utah County, plus Wasatch and Salt Lake counties. There's no need to visit the yard; the bin comes to you.
