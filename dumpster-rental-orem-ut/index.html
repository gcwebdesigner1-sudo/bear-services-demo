Billed on what the load weighs

Delivery and removal includes the first ton. Past that, you pay the scale weight of what's in the bin, so a load of framing scrap costs less than a load of shingles. We can bill that way because every load goes to the Five Mile Pass C&D landfill, which our parent company Dunn Companies owns. You get 14 days on site before a daily fee starts.

Contractor accounts and standing swaps

Running more than one Orem job, or managing a building that fills a bin every week? Open a contractor account once and our office calls you to set up billing. After that, ordering is a call or a text, and a standing rotation means the bin gets swapped on schedule.

Placing a bin in Orem

A jobsite, gravel pad or driveway needs no street permit, as long as the truck has a straight, fairly level run of about 60 feet to set the bin. A street is different. Orem's code doesn't allow anything that blocks a street or sidewalk without a permit, and closing part of a lane or sidewalk goes through the city's Engineering Division with a traffic control plan. If the bin has to sit on the street, call Orem City at (801) 229-7000, ask for Engineering before delivery, and they'll tell you what your spot needs. Our drops and pickups run between 7am and 7pm, inside Orem's hours for container pickups near homes.
