Common questions

      
The things people ask before they book

      
Anything not covered here, call or text (801) 785-4494 — you'll get a straight answer from someone who works here.

    


    

      

        What does it cost?
        
There's a base fee plus the actual weight of what you threw away — and unlike the other guys, we only charge you for the real weight in your bin, not a padded flat rate. Because that depends entirely on your load, we quote it rather than posting a price. Call or text with a rough idea of what's going in and you'll get a number.

      

      

        How long do I get to keep the bin?
        
The standard rental period is 14 days. If you finish early, text us and we'll come pull it. If your project is running long, call or text before the two weeks are up and ask — don't just let it sit.

      

      

        Which size do I need — 15 or 30 yard?
        
The 15 yard (12,000 lbs) handles most cleanouts, remodels, small builds and landscaping loads. The 30 yard (24,000 lbs) is double the capacity for full construction jobs, big demos and commercial volume. If you're on the fence, tell us what you're throwing away and we'll size it for you — that call is free and it's cheaper than a second haul.

      

      

        Can I just text instead of calling?
        
Yes. One text or call does it all — same number, (801) 785-4494. Ordering a bin, scheduling a pickup or asking a question all work over text, and dispatch (Serenity, (801) 404-1017) and field ops (Bryce, (801) 793-2819) take texts on their direct lines too.

      

      

        How soon can you get a bin to me?
        
Depends on what's on the yard that day, so call or text and dispatch will tell you exactly what's open — no guessing games. Quick drop-off and quick pickup are the two things our customers bring up most.

      

      

        Do you do ongoing service, or just one-time?
        
Both. Short-term and long-term rentals, one-time use or a standing rotation for warehouses, shops and docks. Set the schedule once and the bin is there when you need it.

      

      

        Where does my load actually end up?
        
In our own facilities. Bear is part of Dunn Companies, which also runs the Five Mile Pass sand & rock quarry, the Five Mile Pass C&D landfill and American Fork Concrete Recycling. Owning the back end of the trip is exactly why we can bill you on real weight instead of guesswork.

      

      

        What are your hours?
        
8:00am to 4:00pm, Monday through Friday. Texts sent after hours get picked up the next working morning — over 25 years in the waste disposal industry has taught us not to leave people hanging.
