Why Bear

Unlike the other guys, you pay for
the actual weight in your bin

Most companies bill a flat rate whether you filled the bin or barely used it, then start the clock ticking at fourteen days. Bear does neither. You get weighed on what you actually threw away, and you get a month to do it.

Billed on actual weight

We weigh what's in the bin and that's what you pay. No padded flat fee, no paying for air on a half-empty load.

30 days, not 14

Customers keep bringing this one up: most companies give you two weeks and then re-charge the base fee. Bear gives you thirty days.

One text does it all

Don't like calling? Text the same number. Ordering a bin, scheduling the pickup, asking a question — it all works over text.

One-time or ongoing

Long-term and short-term rentals both. Whatever the job requires, one time use or a standing rotation, we have bins ready to go.

What we haul

Three kinds of job, one number

We provide services to commercial and residential construction as well as do-it-yourselfers. Whatever your job requires, we can help.

Construction

Framing scrap, demo debris and jobsite cleanup for builders working single lots or full subdivisions. Bins swapped as fast as the crew fills them.

Commercial

Ongoing service for warehouses, shops and docks. Set a rotation and the bin is there when you need it, gone when it's full.

Residential

Garage cleanouts, remodels, landscaping and everything the do-it-yourselfer drags to the curb. Dropped in the driveway, picked up when you say.
