Common questions

      
The things people ask before they book

      
Anything not covered here, call or text (801) 785-4494 and you'll get a straight answer from someone who works here.

    


    

      

        What does it cost?
        
Your delivery and removal price includes the first ton of trash. After that you're charged by the actual weight in your bin, not a padded flat rate. Rates depend on which city you're in, so call or text (801) 785-4494 and we'll give you the number for your address.

      

      

        How long do I get to keep the bin?
        
You get 14 days before a daily rental fee starts, and it only kicks in after 14 days of inactivity. If you finish early, text us and we'll come pull it. If your project is running long, call or text and we'll tell you what the daily rate looks like for your bin.

      

      

        Which size do I need, 15 or 30 yard?
        
The 15 yard measures 12 ft by 7½ ft by 5½ ft and holds 12,000 lbs. It handles most cleanouts, remodels, small builds and landscaping loads. The 30 yard measures 22 ft by 7½ ft by 6 ft and holds 24,000 lbs, which is double the capacity for full construction jobs, big demos and commercial volume. Measure your driveway before you book, and if you're on the fence, tell us what you're throwing away and we'll size it for you.

      

      

        Can I just text instead of calling?
        
Yes. One text or call does it all at (801) 785-4494. Ordering a bin, scheduling a pickup or asking a question all work over text, and it reaches a person either way.

      

      

        How soon can you get a bin to me?
        
It depends on what's on the yard that day, so call or text and dispatch will tell you exactly what's open. Quick drop-off and quick pickup are the two things our customers bring up most.

      

      

        Do you do ongoing service, or just one-time?
        
Both. Short-term and long-term rentals, one-time use or a standing rotation for warehouses, shops and docks. Set the schedule once and the bin is there when you need it.

      

      

        Where does my load actually end up?
        
In our own facilities. Bear is part of Dunn Companies, which also runs the Five Mile Pass sand & rock quarry and the Five Mile Pass C&D landfill. Owning the back end of the trip is exactly why we can bill you on real weight instead of guesswork.

      

      

        When can I reach you?
        
Call during the day, or text whenever it's convenient. We answer texts outside of working hours too, so if it's easier to send a message at night or over the weekend, send it and we'll pick it up.
