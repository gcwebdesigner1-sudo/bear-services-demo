Why Bear

You pay for the actual weight
in your bin — nothing else

Most companies bill a flat rate whether you filled the bin or barely used it, then start the clock at fourteen days. Bear does neither. You're weighed on what you actually threw away, and you get a month to do it.

Billed on actual weight

We weigh what's in the bin and that's what you pay. No padded flat fee, no paying for air on a half-empty load.

Thirty days, not fourteen

Customers keep bringing this one up: most companies give you two weeks, then charge the base fee again. Bear gives you thirty days.

One text does it all

Don't like calling? Text the same number. Ordering a bin, scheduling a pickup, asking a question — it all works over text.

One-time or ongoing

Short-term and long-term rentals both. One time use or a standing rotation, we have bins ready to go.

What we haul

Three kinds of job, one number

We serve commercial and residential construction as well as do-it-yourselfers. Whatever your job requires, we can help.

Construction

Framing scrap, demo debris and jobsite cleanup for builders working single lots or full subdivisions. Bins swapped as fast as the crew fills them.

Commercial

Ongoing service for warehouses, shops and docks. Set a rotation and the bin is there when you need it, gone when it's full.

Residential

Garage cleanouts, remodels, landscaping and everything the do-it-yourselfer drags to the curb. Dropped in the driveway, picked up when you say.
