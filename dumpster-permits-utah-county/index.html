Street permit guide · checked November 2026

Do you need a permit for a dumpster in Utah County?

Not if the bin sits on a jobsite, a gravel pad or your own driveway. A street is different: most cities need a permit or city permission before a dumpster sits on a public street or sidewalk, and several clear anything left on the street once snow starts. Here is what each city's code says, with the office to call.

Before a bin goes on a street

  • A jobsite, gravel pad or driveway needs no street permit, as long as our truck has a straight, fairly level run of about 60 feet to set the bin.
  • For a public street or sidewalk, call the city office listed below before the drop. Most cities treat a bin on the street like any other obstruction.
  • Keep fire hydrants, storm drains, curb ramps and mailboxes clear.
  • From November through March, plan on the bin sitting off the street so the plows can clear it.

Utah County cities

Alpine

Alpine's code lists trash containers among the things that can't be placed on a sidewalk, park strip, gutter or the street in front of a property, and anything left on a street for 48 hours or more can be removed by police. Call Alpine Engineering and Public Works at (801) 756-6347, ext. 7 before a bin goes on the street. Dumpster rental in Alpine

American Fork

American Fork's code requires a city permit before anything that would obstruct a street goes there, so call American Fork Engineering at (801) 763-3060 before the bin goes on the road. Dumpster rental in American Fork

Eagle Mountain

On a road, yes. Eagle Mountain requires the city's approval before a dumpster goes in a roadway, usually through its Traffic Control Permit, so call (801) 789-6600 and ask for Engineering. Dumpster rental in Eagle Mountain

Highland

Highland's code says anything that blocks a street, gutter, sidewalk or trail needs City Council permission first, and from December 1 to March 31 street parking is banned from midnight to 8am and any time snow is present. Call Highland City Engineering at (801) 772-4515 before a bin goes on the street. Dumpster rental in Highland

Lehi

On a city street, yes. Lehi requires a Right of Way Encroachment Permit with approved traffic control, listed at $500 a week in the city's 2026 to 2027 fee schedule. Call the Lehi Streets Department at (385) 201-1900. Dumpster rental in Lehi

Lindon

For a public street, check with Lindon Public Works at (801) 796-7954 first. In winter, Lindon doesn't allow objects on city streets after a snowfall, so a driveway is the safer spot. Dumpster rental in Lindon

Orem

On a street it depends on the spot. Orem's code bars anything that blocks a street or sidewalk without a permit, so call Orem City at (801) 229-7000 and ask for Engineering before the bin goes on the road. Dumpster rental in Orem

Pleasant Grove

Pleasant Grove requires the city's permission before anything that obstructs a street or sidewalk goes there, so call Pleasant Grove Engineering at (801) 785-5045 before the bin goes on the road. Dumpster rental in Pleasant Grove

Provo

Provo requires a City Engineering permit for work that occupies a public street, so if the bin has to go on the road, call Provo City Engineering at (801) 852-6740 first. Dumpster rental in Provo

Santaquin

Santaquin's code says no roll off container at an apartment, business or mixed use property can sit on a city street, right of way or sidewalk, and at any property it bars refuse containers and building materials from the street out front without City Council permission. Call Santaquin Public Works at (801) 754-1970 before a bin goes on the street. Dumpster rental in Santaquin

Spanish Fork

On a city street, yes. Spanish Fork's code requires written permission from the city before anything is placed in the street, so call Spanish Fork Engineering at (801) 804-4595 first. Dumpster rental in Spanish Fork

Springville

On a public street, yes. Springville's code requires written permission from the Streets Division before a roll off container goes on the road, so call Springville Public Works at (801) 491-2780 first. Dumpster rental in Springville

Vineyard

For a public street, Vineyard requires the city's permission before anything that blocks a street or sidewalk goes there, and items left on a street 48 hours or more can be cited. Call Vineyard Engineering at (801) 226-1929 first. Dumpster rental in Vineyard

Draper

For a public street, check with Draper Engineering at (801) 576-6369. The city's permit for using the public way is an Encroachment Permit, applied for online. Dumpster rental in Draper

Herriman

Herriman's code requires a permit to occupy part of a public street, so call Herriman Engineering at (801) 446-5323 before the bin goes on the road. Dumpster rental in Herriman

Sandy

For a public street, Sandy requires a permit for work in the public way, so call Sandy Public Works at (801) 568-2999 before the bin goes on the road. Dumpster rental in Sandy

Heber City

In Heber City's residential areas a dumpster on the street needs a Special Parking Permit from the Police Department, (435) 654-3040, usually for seven days at most. Outside city limits, Wasatch County wants construction bins kept on the lot. Dumpster rental in Heber City

Permit questions

Questions people ask about street permits

Anything not covered here, call or text (801) 785-4494 and you'll get a straight answer from someone who works here.

Do I need a permit for a dumpster on my driveway?

No. In the cities we serve, a dumpster on your own driveway, a jobsite or a gravel pad needs no street permit, though HOA rules can still apply. The truck needs a straight, fairly level run of about 60 feet to set the bin.

Which cities have winter rules for things left on the street?

Lindon and Eagle Mountain don't allow objects on city streets once snow piles up from November 1 to March 15. Alpine and Highland ban street parking from midnight to 8am from December 1 to March 31 and any time snow is present, Santaquin bans vehicles and trailers on city streets from 11pm to 7am from November 1 to March 31, and Heber City's winter parking rules start November 15.

How much does a street permit for a dumpster cost?

It depends on the city, and many don't list a dumpster fee at all. Lehi's 2026 to 2027 fee schedule lists $500 a week for a dumpster with approved traffic control. Call the city office before the drop for the current fee.

Who do I call about a dumpster on the street?

Usually the city's engineering or public works office. Each city on this page lists the right office and phone number, taken from the city's own website.
