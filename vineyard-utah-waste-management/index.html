Vineyard, Utah

Vineyard, Utah waste management and dumpster rental

Vineyard is one of the fastest-growing cities in Utah, and our yard in Orem is right next door. Roll-off bins for the builders putting up the subdivisions and the homeowners moving into them.

Next door to the fastest-growing city in Utah County

Vineyard went from farmland to a city of subdivisions, townhomes and apartments in a few short years, and it's still building. That means framing scrap, roofing, drywall and landscaping waste on every street. Bear Services has hauled it since the first phases went in. Our yard at 581 W 1600 N in Orem sits just east of the Vineyard line, so a Vineyard address is one of the quickest deliveries we make.

For builders and contractors in Vineyard

The 30 yard is the bin for new construction: 22 feet long, 24,000 lb limit, swapped on a rotation or on a text. We run standing schedules on active subdivisions so the crew never waits on a bin, and every load is billed on scale weight, not a flat per-pull rate. C&D waste goes to the Five Mile Pass C&D landfill, which our parent company Dunn Companies owns.

For Vineyard homeowners

New house, blank yard, unfinished basement: the 15 yard covers the landscaping haul-off, the basement finish and the move-in cleanout. It's 12 feet long and fits a townhome driveway. Text (801) 785-4494 with what you're throwing away and dispatch will size it and give you the Vineyard number. The delivery and removal price includes the first ton, and you have 14 days before a daily rental fee starts.

Placement in Vineyard

Your own driveway is the simple option. Many Vineyard neighborhoods are HOA-managed and have rules on where a container can sit and for how long, so check with the HOA, and check with Vineyard City if the bin has to go on a public street. Tell us the constraints and we'll place it where the truck can reach it.

Vineyard questions

Questions Vineyard customers ask

Anything not covered here, call or text (801) 785-4494 and you'll get a straight answer from someone who works here.

Do you deliver to the new subdivisions in Vineyard?

Yes. Vineyard is one of our closest runs from the Orem yard. Builders get 30 yard rotations; new homeowners finishing a basement or landscaping a lot usually take the 15.

What does a dumpster cost in Vineyard?

Delivery and removal includes the first ton, then you're billed on the actual weight in the bin. Rates are set by city, so text (801) 785-4494 with your Vineyard address for the exact number.

Can the bin go on a townhome or HOA street?

Check with your HOA and Vineyard City first if it can't sit in your own driveway. A container on a public street normally needs a permit, and many HOAs have their own rules on placement and how long it can stay.

Where does the waste go?

To the Five Mile Pass C&D landfill, owned by our parent company Dunn Companies. Owning the landfill is what lets us weigh your load and bill on the real number.
