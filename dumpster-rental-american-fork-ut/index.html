American Fork, Utah

Dumpster rental in American Fork for contractors and businesses

American Fork is one of our shortest runs, about 7 miles from the yard in north Orem. 15 and 30 yard roll off bins for jobsites, remodels and businesses, billed on the real weight of every load.

American Fork work we haul for

New homes and infill lots, remodels and tenant improvements for shops and offices along Main Street and State Street, roofing tear offs, demolition, and businesses that fill a bin every week or two. If it's debris from a jobsite or a building you run, it can go in one of our bins.

Short runs, quick swaps

Our yard at 581 W 1600 N in Orem is about 10 minutes from most of American Fork. When a bin is full, call or text dispatch and the truck doesn't have far to come. That's also why standing rotations work well here: tell us how often the bin fills and we'll swap it on that schedule.

What you pay for

Delivery and removal includes the first ton. Everything past that is the scale weight of the load, so you never pay for air in a half empty bin. It works because every load goes to the Five Mile Pass C&D landfill, owned by our parent company Dunn Companies. You get 14 days on site before a daily fee starts.

Accounts for repeat work

If your crew or your business orders bins often, open a contractor account. Our office calls to set up billing once, and after that every order is just a call or a text.

Placing a bin in American Fork

No permit is needed on a jobsite or private lot. The truck needs a straight, fairly level run of about 60 feet. For a bin on a city street, check with American Fork City before delivery, since most cities in Utah County want a right of way permit for a container on the road.
